  1. Open AIV Eco and choose a model from the AI model menu.

  3. Paste your key. It's stored only in your browser and sent only to that provider.

Questions

Do I need my own API key?

Only for ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Groq. AIV-1 Eco is built in and free to use.

Who pays for my API usage?

Your provider bills your own account for what you use. Chats on your own key don't count toward AIV Eco's free messages.

Is my API key safe?

Your key is stored only in your browser and is sent only to the provider you chose. It doesn't pass through AI Verse.

Does Eco change my model's answers?

Eco picks the model size for each request and keeps answers direct. You can still choose the model and edit which small, medium, and large models it uses.

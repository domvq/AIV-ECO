How the estimates work

AIV Eco models energy from the amount of text the AI model reads and writes, using different rates for small, medium, and large models, plus the web pages fetched for the answer. Water is that energy multiplied by published averages for data-center cooling and electricity generation.

These are estimates, not measurements. AI providers don't publish per-request energy or water for most models, and AIV Eco can't see which data center answered. The estimates are meant to show the direction and size of the savings honestly.

AIV Eco reduces how much computing each answer needs. It can't change how a provider cools its data centers, and its energy and water figures are modeled estimates, not meter readings. How AI uses water

Questions

Is the water number exact?

No. It's a modeled estimate. Real water usage depends on the provider, the data center, its cooling system, and the local power grid.

What does "compared with a traditional approach" mean?

It's the same request sent with the full context to a large AI model. For answers that use the web, it also assumes every page is fully loaded and more text is read.

Does tracking my impact cost extra?

No. The tracker is part of every answer and is free.

Where are my totals stored?

Your impact totals are kept in your browser on this device.

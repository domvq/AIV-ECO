The eco-friendly AI assistant that uses less water

AIV Eco is an AI assistant built around one idea: give you the answer you need with the least computing possible. Less computing means less electricity, and less electricity means less water used to cool data centers and generate power. Every answer shows its estimated energy and water usage, so you can see the difference.

How AIV Eco uses less water

No AI when it isn't needed

Math, unit conversions, and the date and time are worked out right on your device. No data center, no water.

Reuses answers it already has

Ask a general question that was already answered on your device, and AIV Eco shows the saved answer instead of running the AI again.

The right-size model

Simple requests go to a small model and hard ones to a large one, instead of sending everything to the biggest model.

Efficient web reading

AIV Eco reads web pages as plain text first, reuses pages it has already read, and stops searching once the question is answered.

Shorter, direct answers

Eco mode answers directly, without padding. Fewer words generated means less computing.

Your impact, on every answer

Each answer shows estimated energy and water, and Your impact keeps a running total of what you've saved.

Everything you expect from an AI assistant

Using less water doesn't mean doing less. AIV Eco chats, searches the web, does deep research, reads your PDFs and documents, writes and runs code, creates images and short videos, and makes documents, spreadsheets, and presentations. It remembers what you ask it to, works with voice, and opens the AI Verse apps for health, study, finance, careers, and more.

Prefer a model you already know? Pick ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Groq with your own API key, and AIV Eco still applies its water-saving steps around every request.

How it works

  1. Open aiv-x.com/eco. No account needed to start.
  2. Ask anything. AIV Eco decides the lightest way to answer well: on your device, from a saved answer, or with the smallest AI model that fits.
  3. Check the energy and water line under the answer, or open Your impact to see your totals.

AIV Eco reduces how much computing each answer needs. It can't change how a provider cools its data centers, and its energy and water figures are modeled estimates, not meter readings. How AI uses water

Questions

Does AIV Eco really use less water?

AIV Eco uses less computing for the same task by skipping work an answer doesn't need, and computing is what drives AI's energy and water usage. Its per-answer figures are estimates based on that computing.

Is AIV Eco free?

Yes. You can send 10 messages without an account, and a free account includes 20 messages a day. Chats that use your own ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Groq key don't count toward the free messages.

Is AIV Eco as capable as other AI assistants?

It has the features of AIV X, AI Verse's full AI assistant, including web search, deep research, file reading, images, video, documents, voice, and memory. Eco mode keeps answers direct.

Who makes AIV Eco?

AIV Eco is made by AI Verse, a California AI company that builds AI apps for everyday tasks.
